LoanPlan Privacy Policy

Smart Loan & EMI Calculator

Effective date: October 7, 2026  |  App: LoanPlan  |  Package: com.abuhurraira.loanplan0

In short: LoanPlan does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal information. The app works fully offline on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the LoanPlan Android application ("LoanPlan", "the app") handles information. The app is published by Abu Hurraira ("we", "us", "our").

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. LoanPlan does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app convenient, LoanPlan saves the most recent values you entered (loan amount, interest rate, tenure and extra monthly payment) in the app's private storage on your device. This data:

  • stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else;
  • is used only to pre-fill the calculator the next time you open the app;
  • is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Depending on your device settings, Android's built-in backup feature may include app data in your own Google account backup. This is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

4. Permissions

LoanPlan requests no permissions. It does not access the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or any other protected feature of your device.

5. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social media or any other third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to any server.

6. Data sharing and sale

Because we do not receive any of your data, we do not share, sell, rent or disclose it to any third party.

7. Summary of data practices

Personal data collectedNone
Data shared with third partiesNone

8. Children's privacy

LoanPlan is a general finance utility and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information, no data is collected from children either.

9. Security

Your inputs are processed locally on your device and are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side data for us to protect or lose.

10. Financial disclaimer

LoanPlan provides estimates for information purposes only, based on a fixed interest rate and equal monthly payments. It is not financial advice and it does not offer, arrange or process loans. Actual EMI, fees, charges and terms depend on your lender, so please confirm details with them before making a decision.

11. Your choices

You can delete all data saved by the app at any time by clearing its storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
